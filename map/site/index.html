How it works: an NVIDIA Earth-2 weather model forecasts the next three days. The Hot-Dry-Windy Index and recent fire detections feed a model trained on 2019 to 2022 fire seasons. Water and cells without terrain data are not shown.

Sources: fire detections from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS); weather forecast start from NOAA GFS; forecast from NVIDIA Earth2Studio; elevation from Copernicus DEM GLO-30; land cover from ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
